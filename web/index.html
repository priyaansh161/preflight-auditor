Landing page pre-flight auditor

Is this page ready for ad money?

A robot shopper opens the page in a real browser, acts like someone arriving from an ad, and checks that Google Analytics, Meta and Google Ads are told the truth about what it did: once, at the right price, credited to the right ad.

1It shops

Lands from a fake ad click, picks a size, adds to cart, starts checkout. Never buys.

2It listens

Records every message the page sends to the ad platforms, without letting them reach the real accounts.

3It compares

Checks those messages against what really happened and writes a plain-English report.
