HighDouble countingOwner: Marketing / ads teamNeeds confirmation

Two Meta pixels both report "ViewContent"

What we saw. Each time the shopper viewed the product, the event is sent to 2 pixels: 223335195137418 (via tmgoogletagmanager) and 558552204508330 (via shopify_web_pixel).

Why it matters. If both pixels sit in the same ad account or dataset, Meta counts each conversion twice and reported ROAS is overstated. If they belong to separate accounts (e.g. an agency), this is intentional.

What to do. Confirm in Meta Events Manager which pixel your campaigns optimise on; retire the other or remove it from this site.

5 uncaught JavaScript errors on the page

What we saw. Script errors were thrown: 1 during "Scrolling through the page", 2 during "Tapping the first product in the list", 1 during "Tapping "Add to cart" before choosing a size", 1 during "Starting checkout (no order placed)".

Why it matters. A crashing script can stop a tag (or a button) from working for some shoppers. None broke the tested journey, but they make the page fragile.

What to do. Have a developer trace each error to its script and fix or remove it.

MediumDouble countingOwner: Analytics

Extra page views counted with no new page (choosing an in-stock size / adding the item to the cart)

What we saw. No new page loaded, but 6 page-view hits went out (Meta Pixel 223335195137418, Meta Pixel 558552204508330, Google Ads AW-766814719, Google Analytics 4 G-3042L5GP9T, Google Analytics 4 G-5RYZDGXEFH, Google Analytics 4 G-VDE0ZFZ4LP). The theme rewrites the address bar (history change) when a size is picked, and the tags treat that as a new page.

Why it matters. Page views and sessions are inflated and engagement rate / bounce rate are distorted. Meta also receives extra PageView signals.

What to do. In GA4 turn off "page changes based on browser history events" (or exclude this theme's replaceState), and remove History Change triggers from page-view tags in GTM.

LowPage & shopper experienceOwner: Marketing / ads team

90 outside domains load on this page (28+ separate vendors)

What we saw. 656 requests went to 90 outside domains; 28 vendors could be named, the rest are unidentified (full list in the appendix).

Why it matters. Each vendor adds load time and another script that can break. Old, unused vendors are common in this list.

What to do. Review the vendor list with marketing and remove anything no one is using.

LowTags present & firingOwner: DeveloperNeeds confirmation

Google Ads: no "begin_checkout" event when the shopper started checkout

What we saw. The shopper started checkout, but Google Ads never received "begin_checkout". A classic conversion tag did fire at this step (label ubtwCIegxvoaEP_T0u0C).

Why it matters. Probably covered by the classic conversion tag; only matters if remarketing audiences rely on the standard event.

What to do. Add or fix the "begin_checkout" tag/trigger for this step.

LowTags present & firingOwner: Analytics

GA4 does not record which product was tapped in the list

What we saw. No "select_item" event when the shopper opened a product from the collection.

Why it matters. List click-through rates per product are unavailable.

What to do. Fire select_item on product-card clicks.

LowDouble countingOwner: Analytics

Same action tracked under two event names in GA4

What we saw. Alongside the standard events, GA4 also receives: ADD-TO-CART-GA4-V2 (≈ add_to_cart).

Why it matters. Harmless on its own, but if someone marks the custom one as a key event or imports it into Google Ads, the action is counted twice.

What to do. Retire the legacy custom events, or make sure only the standard event is a key event.

LowPrivacy & consentOwner: Marketing / ads teamNeeds confirmation

All tracking is switched on before the visitor agrees to anything

What we saw. Consent Mode default is "granted" for ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization, analytics_storage, and no consent banner changed it.

Why it matters. Good for measurement: nothing is held back. The risk is regulatory: India's DPDP Act requires consent for processing personal data, with its rules being phased in. A legal call, not a launch blocker.

What to do. Confirm the policy with legal; if a banner is added later, re-run this audit to make sure tags still fire after "Accept".
